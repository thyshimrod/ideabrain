1. Dance in the kitchen for one song.  
Not because you’re good dancers.  
Because it’s almost impossible to stay emotionally disconnected while you’re laughing together.  
  
2. Flirt when there’s zero chance it’ll lead to sex.  
Walk by and whisper something in their ear.  
Wink across the room.  
Grab their butt while they’re making dinner. 😉  
Playfulness creates anticipation, not pressure.  
  
3. Bring back one thing you did while you were dating.  
Mini golf.  
Late-night ice cream.  
Watching the sunset.  
Couples drift when they stop dating each other.  
  
4. Create one ridiculous inside joke.  
The happiest couples I know have their own language.  
The joke isn’t the point.  
It’s creating a little world that only the two of you share.  
  
5. Turn ordinary errands into adventures.  
Get drinks and drive with no destination.  
Race grocery carts to the car.  
Listen to your high school playlist while folding laundry.  
Fun is a mindset, not a budget.  
  
6. Touch each other every day without asking for anything.  
Hold hands on a walk.  
Rest your hand on their leg in the car.  
Scratch their back while you’re watching TV.  
Affection says, “I still like being close to you.”  
  
7. Make it your mission to hear your spouse laugh every day.  
Not just smile.  
Laugh.  
  
That might be the most underrated relationship goal there is.  
  
The opposite of roommate syndrome isn’t just better communication.  
  
It’s genuinely enjoying each other again.  
  
❤️ Save this for the week life feels way too serious.  
  
Then send it to your spouse and ask:  
  
“Which one are we trying first?”
