**Note 1**

> Understanding a relational pattern and seeing yourself inside it in real time are two completely different skills.
> 
> Most personal development builds the first one. Almost none of it builds the second.

---

**Note 2**

> The moment you enter the game isn't a moment of ignorance.
> 
> It's a moment of automatism. Which means knowing what's happening doesn't stop it from happening.

---

**Note 3**

> The role you play preferentially in a relationship is the one that feels most like reality.
> 
> Not like a role. Like the obvious response to what's happening.
> 
> That's exactly what makes it so hard to catch — and so costly to keep playing.

---

**Note 4**

> One second is enough.
> 
> Not to resolve the scene. Not to have a different conversation. Just to pause the automatic and introduce a choice.
> 
> That pause, practiced over time, is what actually changes the pattern. Not the insight. The pause.

---

**Note 5**

> Most people try to exit relational patterns by understanding them better.
> 
> They end up with more precise descriptions of what they keep doing. The pattern stays exactly where it was.
