**Note 2**

> The case file doesn't announce itself. It just gets thicker.
> 
> Entry by entry, conversation by conversation — while you continue to show up as someone who communicates well.

---

**Note 3**

> A question worth sitting with today :
> 
> The last time something landed badly and you said nothing — where did it go?
> 
> Not the thought you had later. The thing that moved through you in the moment, before you decided not to say it.

---

**Note 4**

> "I say nothing because there's no point defending myself. But internally I'm taking note. It's not the first time and it won't be the last."
> 
> Nobody around that person knows what's in the file. Including, sometimes, the person themselves.

---

**Note 5**

> The Persecutor in someone who's done real work on themselves doesn't look like cruelty.
> 
> It looks like restraint. Like perspective. Like someone who has learned not to react.
> 
> That's what makes it so hard to catch.

---

**Note 6**

> What the silent case file costs isn't the relationship. It's trust — without either person knowing exactly why it's eroding.
> 
> The other person adjusts. Measures what they say. Takes fewer risks. The relationship doesn't break. It shrinks.

---

**Note 7**

> The real question underneath the file isn't "how do I say this better?"
> 
> It's : _what am I actually risking if I say it at all?_
> 
> The file is control. Saying it means letting that go.
