
Pour les notes, le principe est simple : courtes, autonomes, pas promotionnelles. Une idée, une tension, une question — le lecteur doit avoir envie de répondre ou de partager. Le lien vers le guide n'apparaît pas dans chaque note — seulement dans une ou deux, naturellement.

Voici 7 notes pour la première semaine — une par jour, autour du thème de l'article 1.

Lancement :
New essay out today — and it starts with something uncomfortable.

You've done the work. You know the frameworks. You can name what's happening in your relationship in real time. And yet last week, or last night, you ended up in the same scene again.

This isn't about doing more work. It's about a specific gap that most relational frameworks don't address — the distance between understanding a pattern and actually seeing yourself inside it, in the moment, when it still matters.

If that gap sounds familiar, this one's for you.

_(link to article)_

**Jour 1 — Publication de l'article**

> You can name the dynamic, explain the pattern, see it coming — and still end up inside it by evening. Understanding isn't the same as seeing. That gap is where most relational work gets stuck. _(link to article)_

---

**Jour 2**

> The hardest role to spot in a relationship isn't the one that causes damage. It's the one that looks like effort.

---

**Jour 3**

> A question worth sitting with: In your last difficult moment with your partner — what was your first internal reaction? Not the one you expressed. The one that came before that.

---

**Jour 4**

> Most relational frameworks help you understand what's happening. Very few help you catch the moment you enter it. That's a different problem — and it needs a different tool.

---

**Jour 5**

> "I was really listening. I was there." "I should have stayed quiet." "Whatever I do, it's never the right thing." Three people. Same scene. None of them wrong about what they felt.

---

**Jour 6**

> The partner who thinks they're not the problem is often the one making the most effort. That's not a coincidence.

---

**Jour 7 — Relance douce vers le guide**

> If something in this week's article landed — the guide goes further. Six scenes. Your dominant role. One intervention. 15€. One sitting. _(link to Gumroad)_
