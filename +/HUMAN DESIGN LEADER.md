
**Purpose of the Human Design Lead  
**The main purpose of the Human Design Lead is to be the single point of accountability for the successful enablement and implementation of culture / ways of working transformation in our clients. Human Design activities align to Global Practices and include:  
  
  

- Human enablement
- Behavioural transformation
- Leadership Development Programs
- Coaching
- Learning  
      
      
      
    

**What You’ll Do**  
  
  

- New and existing senior clients’ relationships

- Actively involved in the end to end cycle of new clients’ pursuits, sales and onboarding
- Responsible for regional client portfolio (expected approx 4 clients)
- Point of contact for senior client relationships long term
- C-suite leadership coaching and enablement  
      
    

- Kick-start Human Design discussion with clients

- Initial sprint setup including leading activities such as

- Line of Sight
- Culture survey
- Exec LDP  
      
    

- Liaise with Human Design Global Practice Leads to influence and bring best practice into programs
- Lead top-down cultural transformation, working with the C-suite and exec layer in organisations
- Senior cover for Human Design activities throughout migration including ongoing risk monitoring and escalation  
      
    

- Cultural stewardship

- Embody the Kraken ways of working and support the delivery team to do the same (and ensure a consistent experience across the portfolio)
- Act as the bridge between new clients and best practice success stories  
      
      
      
    

**What You’ll Deliver**  
  
  

- In depth knowledge and expertise in all facets of Human Design necessary to enable transformation (including cultural transformation, leadership development, coaching, learning)
- Experience in leading portfolios of major program transformations across different geographies
- Track record of trust-based partnerships with C-suite and executives
- Excellent communication skills at storytelling level across different seniorities and cultures
- Excellent facilitation skills
- Experience in navigating ambiguity and complexity
- Clear understanding of the connection between human, organisation and technology in the context of tech-enabled transformations
- Experience of working within the Energy, Utilities and Telco industries is highly desirable
- In depth knowledge of leadership and organisation behavioral best practices including concepts such as

- Psychological safety
- Empowerment
- Growth mindset
- Agile leadership
- High performing team  
      
      
      
    

**What We’re Looking For  
  
**

- A systems thinker who is human-led based in Paris, France
- We are a kind, friendly, human organisation
- Deep understanding of adult learning, behaviour change and organisational psychology
- A world-class and experienced leadership development expert
- A storyteller who deeply understands the art of facilitation
- Proven credibility working with senior leaders and executives
- Experience building or scaling leadership or learning practices in complex environments
- Calm, confident and playful presence - able to challenge with empathy and clarity
